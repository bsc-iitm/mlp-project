MLP Project Viva Guidelines

The following document outlines the comprehensive guidelines, timelines, evaluation scheme, and academic integrity policies for the MLP Project Viva examinations. All students must read and adhere to these guidelines.

ImportantEligibility at a glance

To become eligible for the Viva examinations, students must be successfully registered and obtain a submission score on the Kaggle Leaderboard greater than 0.70 before the provided deadlines.

Timelines and Deadlines

The deadlines to cross the Kaggle cutoff and complete your Vivas depend on the term in which you have completed or are completing the MLP theory course. The MLP theory course is considered as completed if you have already obtained a passing grade (includes grades S,A,B,C,D,E).

MLP Course Theory Completion Term Deadline to Cross Cutoff Deadline to Complete Both Vivas Evaluation Scheme
Jan 2026 and earlier Nov 15, 2026 Dec 6, 2026 Evaluated out of 100 marks.
May 2026 Nov 15, 2026 Dec 6, 2026 Evaluated out of 105 marks.
Sep 2026 Nov 22, 2026 Dec 20, 2026 Evaluated out of 105 marks if Viva is completed in Batch 1.
Note

The deadlines mentioned above are to cross the cut-off score. All students, regardless of batch, may continue to make submissions to improve their Kaggle Leaderboard score until the end of the competition (Nov 22, 2026).

Viva Batches

The Viva examinations will be conducted in 2 batches. The bonus marks provided and the Viva reattempt policy is subject to the batch.

Batch Level 1 Level 2 Level 1 Reattempt
Batch 1 Nov 9 onwards Nov 17 onwards Allowed
Batch 2 Dec 6 onwards Not Allowed

The Level 2 Viva examination will not have a reattempt option in both the batches. Students who fail the Level 2 Viva examination will have to register and pay the fees to repeat the entire project in the next term.

Note

The batches for Viva examination completion too are based on the MLP theory course completion term. Students who have already obtained a passing grade in May 2026 term or earlier have to complete both the Level 1 and Level 2 Viva examinations in Batch 1. Students completing the MLP theory course in Sep 2026 term can complete their Viva in either Batch 1 or Batch 2. The bonus component for students doing the MLP theory course in Sep 2026 term is applicable only if they complete their Viva in Batch 1.

Academic Malpractice

The project will be considered complete only once the final grade for the project course is published on the student dashboard.

Any inappropriate conduct during the project period may be treated as malpractice. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Sharing code snippets, notebooks, or any project-related material with any other students. This includes students not registered for the project course.
  • Submitting work that is wholly or partially copied from another student.
  • Allowing another individual to complete any portion of the project on one’s behalf.
  • Using solutions, code repositories, or notebooks from external sources.
  • Reproducing project outputs that are substantially similar to another student’s work, even if variable names or minor details are altered.

Penalties

All parties flagged under academic malpractice will be subject to the following penalties:

  • ‘U’ grade in all the courses in the concerned term
  • Debarred from registering for courses in the subsequent 2 terms

Academic integrity reminder

Students should ensure that all project work, code, notebooks, analyses, and outputs are their own and comply with the stated academic integrity requirements.

Level 1 Viva

The Level-1 Viva is designed to ensure that each participant in the Kaggle Project has put in a significant amount of effort in their MLP Project. This ensures that only participants who have shown dedication and effort in their Project work are eligible for the Level-2 Viva.

Important

Completing all items on the checklist is mandatory.

Level 1 Viva Checklist

The following components will be tested in the Level 1 viva:

  1. Problem statement and dataset
    • The problem statement is understood correctly.
    • The dataset is appropriately loaded.
  2. End-to-end analytical reasoning
    • The focus throughout the notebook from EDA → Preprocessing → Feature Engineering → Model Building → Error / Performance Analysis is to highlight the insights and explain the influence of each step on the subsequent approach.
  3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
    • Objective: Understand key statistics and relationships in the dataset.
    • Includes univariate and bivariate feature analysis on the relevant features.
    • Use appropriate visualizations that are clear, readable, and relevant.
  4. Systematic preprocessing of the data
    • Detection and appropriate handling of missing values.
    • Need for appropriate scaling of numerical features.
    • Need for appropriate encoding of categorical features.
    • All data transformations (data cleaning, scaling, encoding, etc.) should be implemented using reusable pipelines or custom functions and consistently applied to both training and test datasets to avoid data leakage, ensure reproducibility, and reduce redundant code.
  5. Feature Engineering / Extraction
    • Creation of new features / transformation of existing features followed by analysis of their importance.
  6. Model experimentation and hyperparameter tuning
    • Experiment with multiple models from different families followed by hyperparameter tuning to optimize performance.
  7. Model comparison
    • Compare the performance of at least three different models on training / validation data using appropriate evaluation metrics.
    • Highlight the advantages and disadvantages of each model.
  8. Notebook quality
    • The notebook should be clean, well-structured, appropriately commented, and contain the relevant output.
  9. Best-performing model
    • The notebook submission should contain the best-performing model.
Note

The Level-1 viva serves as a check to see if minimum requirements are met before attempting Level-2. The final scores will depend only on the Level-2 performance.

Repeat L1 Viva

If a student does not meet the minimum requirements in the Level-1 viva, they will get another chance to clear the Level-1 viva provided it is attempted in Batch 1.

Level 2 Viva

How to Prepare for Level 2 Viva Voce Examination

Kaggle Notebook

You are expected to clearly explain the code you have written and logically describe your choice of:

  • Data preprocessing steps
  • Modeling approach
  • Inspection of underfitting / overfitting

The viva examiner may ask you to briefly (5 minutes or less) walk through your Kaggle notebook.

Scope of Questions

The viva examiner may ask questions from any course you have taken for your DS diploma.

The Kaggle competition may belong to only, say, classification, yet the examiner can ask questions from:

  • Regression
  • Unsupervised machine learning
  • All topics learned in courses till MLP
  • MLF
  • MLT
  • Statistics
  • Other relevant course topics

Internal Working of Models

Only using an API (e.g. DecisionTreeClassifier, SVC, etc.) is not sufficient. You should also know how the model works internally.

For every machine learning model that you have learnt and/or used in your Kaggle notebook, you must know and be able to explain its 5 components:

Component What you should know
1. Data The data used by the model
2. Model The mathematical / algorithmic model
3. Loss function How prediction error is measured
4. Optimization procedure / training How the model learns its parameters
5. Evaluation How model performance is assessed

Programming During the Viva

You may be asked to:

  • Write code from scratch.
  • Make changes to your existing code during the viva examination.

Level 2 Viva Marking Scheme

For Level 2 viva, students are expected to be well-versed with the following details of the components mentioned in the subsequent table:

Area Expected Knowledge
1. Conceptual understanding Clear understanding of the underlying concepts
2. Mathematical details Ability to explain relevant mathematical foundations
3. Practical aspect Ability to apply concepts to practical problems
4. Programming Ability to implement and modify solutions in code

Repeat Level 2 Viva-voce Examination

In case a student scores between 15–24/50 marks, they may be passed with D / E grade at the instructor’s discretion.

If a student scores <15/50 in the viva examination, they have to:

  1. Register and pay the fees.
  2. Repeat the entire project the next term.
  3. Become eligible to attend the viva.

It is seen that such students need more practice and time to study the fundamentals again.
